## POWER BI PROJECT: MADHAV ECOMMERCE SALES DASHBOARD

## Key Insights

1. **Maharashtra Generated the Highest Profit**
   Maharashtra emerged as the most profitable state, generating the highest overall profit among all states. **Madhya Pradesh** ranked second, indicating strong business performance and customer demand in these two markets.

2. **Harivansh Was the Top Customer by Purchase Value**
   **Harivansh** was identified as the highest-value customer, with total purchases of approximately **₹10,000**. This indicates a significant contribution from this customer to overall sales revenue.

3. **Clothing Dominated the Product Categories**
   The **Clothing** category accounted for approximately **63% of the total market**, making it the dominant product category. This suggests that clothing products represent a major portion of customer demand and sales volume.

4. **Printers Were the Top-Selling Sub-Category**
   Among all product sub-categories, **Printers** recorded the highest sales, making them the leading sub-category across the analyzed markets. This highlights printers as an important contributor to overall product sales.

5. **Credit Card and Cash on Delivery Were the Most Used Payment Methods**
   **Credit Card and Cash on Delivery (COD)** were the most frequently used payment methods among customers. This indicates a strong preference for both digital card-based payments and payment-at-delivery options.

6. **Madhav Store Generated ₹37,000 in Total Profit**
   **Madhav Store** generated approximately **₹37,000 in total profit** across all states included in the analysis, demonstrating its overall contribution to the business's profitability.
